Geerts and Steyaert finished loop 124 together and stopped there. To win a backyard ultra, one runner has to go further than everyone else, so both are listed as DNF. The record still stands. Before this race, Steyaert's best was 113 loops (Big's 2025) and Geerts's best was 110 (World Team Championships 2024). Phil Gore's 119 loops at Dead Cow Gully in June 2025 is still the most by a race winner.

In August 2026, Phil Gore ran 151 loops alone in Project 168. It was not an official race, so it is not recognized as a record.

The assist is the runner who completes one fewer loop than the winner. Because the format allows only one winner, the second-place finisher is classified as a DNF, but the assist is still tracked as a record category in its own right.

Notable firsts

2011
First Big's winner
Tim Englund USA

The inaugural Big Dog's Backyard Ultra was held in Bell Buckle, Tennessee in October 2011. Englund completed 18 loops to win the format's first world championship event, with Dave Carver as the assist on 17.

2019
First female overall winner
Maggie Guterl USA

Guterl became the first woman to win an outright backyard ultra world championship, completing 60 loops at Big's 2019, outlasting the entire men's field in the process.

2022
First runners to break 100 loops
Merijn Geerts & Ivo Steyaert BEL

At the 2022 Backyard Ultra World Team Championships in Belgium, Geerts and Steyaert completed 101 loops (420 miles), the first time two runners had broken the 100-loop barrier in the sport's history. The pair mutually chose to stop rather than continue competing against each other.

Individual world champions at Big's

Big Dog's Backyard Ultra is held in October in Bell Buckle, Tennessee, at the farm of Lazarus Lake. It ran every year until 2019. Since 2020 it hosts the individual world championship in odd years. In even years the world team championship takes its place.

World team champions

In even years, each country runs its own race at home and all races start at the same time. The loops of every runner on a team are added up. The country with the most loops wins.

Year Winning team Team loops Most loops by one runner
2026 USA 1,120 Merijn Geerts & Ivo Steyaert (BEL), 124
2024 Belgium 1,147 Merijn Geerts, Ivo Steyaert & Frank Gielen (BEL), 110
2022 USA 860 Merijn Geerts & Ivo Steyaert (BEL), 101
2020 Belgium 574 Karel Sabbe (BEL), 75

The last runner in each country wins that country's race, but there is no individual world champion in a team year. In 2026 Mark Dowdle won the USA race at Big's with 107 loops, and Jon Noll was the assist with 106.
