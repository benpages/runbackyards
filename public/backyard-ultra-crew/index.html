Most crews are winging it

Look around the tents at any backyard ultra. Most crews are friends and family who came to help. They care about you more than anyone there. They have also never done this before, so they run on good intentions and no system.

So things get missed. The food goes on too late. Nobody is sure who is doing what. You end up making the calls yourself between loops, which is the one thing you cannot afford to spend energy on.

At loop 12 none of that costs you anything. At loop 30 it ends your race.

What changes when we are in your tent

We do not write your race plan. You have one. We study it, we go through your preferences with you, and we share our experience where it helps.

Then during the race our systems keep things tight and crisp, so all you have to worry about is running. That means:

  • Your fueling is on point.
  • You get the maximum amount of rest each loop.
  • Your tent stays organized.
  • You are not making decisions on behalf of your crew.
  • Everyone in the tent has a clear role.

Who this is for

  • You have run a backyard before and you know what the format does to you.
  • You are going for 40 hours or more, or you are going for the win.
  • You want people in your tent who have done this before.

If this is your first backyard, start with our free crewing tips and hand them to whoever is looking after you. That will get you a long way.

Common questions

What does it cost?

We work that out with you once we have talked. It depends on the race, where it is, and how many of us come.

Do I pay for travel?

Travel is part of the same conversation. Nothing is fixed, and we would rather agree it with you than put a number on a page.

Can I choose who comes?

Tell us if you have a preference. Once we have your date we will tell you who is free, and whether that is one of us or all three.

Can you work alongside my own crew?

Yes, and it is often the best setup. We are happy to work alongside whichever family and friends you want there, with clear roles agreed in advance.

Where do you crew?

In principle, anywhere. We are based in Europe, so tell us where your race is and we will be straight with you about what is realistic.

What if you say no?

We will tell you why, and point you at the free crewing tips so your own crew can run a tighter race.
